HMAC-SHA256 Lab

Two people, one shared secret, and an email that anyone could tamper with. The sender seals a message with HMAC and emails it. The receiver checks the seal. Open this site on two devices, pick a role on each, and watch tampering get caught.

How the demo flows

  1. Agree on a key first. The sender and receiver share a secret key through a channel that is not the email: say it aloud, or send it in a chat app.
  2. Sender seals and emails. The email carries the message and its MAC, never the key.
  3. Receiver pastes the email into the receiver page and enters the key.
  4. Verification. Untouched, it reads Authentic. Edit one character of the message before verifying, as an attacker in the middle would, and it reads Not authentic.
